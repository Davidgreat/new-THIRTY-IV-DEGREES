CREATE ONE INTAKE WITH SEVERAL PROGRAMMES
Training Admin → Programmes & intakes.
Enable shared intakes once if prompted. Shared training demos prepare this automatically.
Add reusable programmes, then Create an intake: enter its name, training dates and shared application closing date. Check one or several programmes and set a learner capacity for each.
Open the shared intake to add another programme, update its shared application deadline, or change the enrolment window for all included programmes.
Programme search keeps a long programme list manageable.

EXTEND AN INTAKE YOU ALREADY CREATED
Programmes & intakes → Existing single-programme intakes → open the intake → Add programmes to this intake.
It becomes a shared intake without moving students, replacing admission numbers, rewriting historical certificates, or changing its existing subscription/support records.
Open that shared intake → Add another programme.
Unconverted single-programme intakes continue working as before. This update does not merge different existing paid intakes or move their records automatically.

WHAT IS SHARED / WHAT STAYS SEPARATE
Shared: intake name, training dates, application deadline, subscription seat pool, enrolment-window controls and an optional intake notice audience.
Separate per programme: learner capacity, applications/questions/fees, courses, trainers, teaching attendance, results, certificates, sponsorship/stipend rules and internships.
The existing centre application link remains the entry point. Applicants choose the programme/intake. Application forms still need to be configured/enabled for each programme; the shared deadline applies automatically.
Noticeboard → Publish a notice → choose everyone or one shared intake. An intake notice reaches enrolled trainees across its programmes plus active centre staff/trainers. Recipients are saved at publication; opening a notice does not acknowledge it.

SUBSCRIPTION SEATS
Fees & subscriptions presents one checkout per shared intake, not a separate charge per included programme.
A learner account enrolled in two included programmes counts once against the shared purchased seats. Historical enrolments retain the seat-use protection; duplicate accounts are distinct accounts and must be reviewed by the centre.
Each programme still enforces its own learner capacity. Configuring/adding a programme does not purchase seats or automatically increase capacity. Top up the intake allocation when needed.
The existing centre platform rate and duration choices still determine the quote. Learner course fees remain separate from the centre subscription.
Teaching access/expiry follows the shared subscription; own historical records and stipend wallets remain available according to existing access rules.

STIPEND SETUP
Sponsors & stipends → Set up sponsor & stipends, or open a programme in a shared intake → Sponsor & stipend rules.
Enter that programme cohort’s sponsor and enable stipends when applicable.
Training stipend: choose No training stipend, Weekly training stipend OR Fixed training stipend. Only the selected amount box opens; the other is disabled and ignored server-side. Weekly and fixed cannot both be enabled for new terms.
Internship stipend: independently check Monthly internship stipend to open its amount and required approved log-day fields.
Required documents: select NIN — National Identification Number; LASRRA — Lagos State Residents Registration Agency number; both; or neither. Required evidence must pass review before payment.
Bank detail setup remains after successful enrolment/login, never in the public application form. Existing conventional-bank restrictions, document privacy, payment review, scheduled exports, manual settlement and Paystack verification remain in place.
A shared intake can have a different sponsor and stipend arrangement for each programme.
Existing terms with earned balances remain locked, including older weekly+fixed arrangements. You can update the sponsor or pause new earnings without changing those financial commitments. Use new cohorts for new financial terms.
Fully sponsored free courses still require a zero course fee in Admissions; sponsor naming does not rewrite old fee quotes or waive centre subscription costs.
