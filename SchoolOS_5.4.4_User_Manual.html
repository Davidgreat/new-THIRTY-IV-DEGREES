About this manual

SchoolOS Nigeria 5.4.4. Updated 17 November 2026 (WAT). This manual describes current implemented workflows, role boundaries and provider-dependent services. Menus vary with school services, assignments and record links. Read the short Start here guide first; use role chapters as needed. There are 35 staff job profiles and 41 demo identities. Production schools activate only the jobs they need. No guide grants access or replaces school policy, safeguarding, accounting or legal review.

Start here: your first school day — Know the three kinds of access

The Platform Super Admin operates SchoolOS for participating schools. A School Admin sets up and controls one school. Staff, learners and parents work in their own school accounts. A job profile is a responsibility, not a separate school or a universal administrator. A school with five employees can combine suitable responsibilities on existing logins; a larger school can split duties and scopes. Activate only the services the school actually operates.

Start here: your first school day — Start a new school

Use Start your school. Enter the school and owner details, choose an available plan and billing cycle, and review the configured price/trial. Sign in to the created owner account and complete any required subscription step. Prices, trials and availability come from platform settings; they are not identical for every school. A payment-return page alone is not verification. An administratively suspended school needs platform review even after paying.

Start here: your first school day — Use Start my school

The School Admin home has Start my school, My school day and More tools. Start my school shows six saved-record checks: Your school, Your classes, Your teaching team, Safe access, Your pupils and Connect families. Pick the highlighted next step and follow the short instruction. The percentage updates from actual records. Listen to this step is optional browser/device voice with a Stop control; no pupil names or balances are read aloud.

Start here: your first school day — Know what can wait

Open What can wait? for fees, assessments, dates/calendar, email delivery, extra jobs/services and school policy work. These do not reduce the six-step percentage, but must be configured before you rely on the related service. Review privacy and retention before wider pupil-data use. A saved access-plan acknowledgement records the owner’s plan; it is not legal certification or approval of every formal school policy.

Start here: your first school day — Bring an existing school on board

Create current classes and sessions first. Use Admissions → existing families / Family onboarding for the school-specific parent registration link. A parent supplies their own contact details and one or more children. The school reviews each child against existing records, matches an existing pupil or approves a new record, and confirms the class. Manual family entry is also available. Do not process an existing enrolled child as a fresh applicant just to obtain a login.

Start here: your first school day — Bring a new pupil into the school

Use Admissions for an enquiry or application where needed, and the pupil registration/admission workflow for actual intake. Review required details/documents, fees if the school requires them, class placement and the admission decision. Approval creates or connects the pupil record. Family & login setup then creates or connects verified parent/learner access. An unreviewed public form never creates unrestricted access to children’s records.

Start here: your first school day — Connect the first teacher

Open My team and add a staff member. Select Teacher, verify identity and employment, and connect an existing HR/teacher record when one already exists. Give the teacher their current-session classes and subjects in Academics. Hand over the temporary sign-in privately. Test that teacher’s view before inviting the whole team. An HR record, directory entry and login are connected records with different purposes.

Start here: your first school day — Test one complete family

Approve one parent and all their verified child links. Share each person’s own sign-in. Check the parent sees only their children, the pupil sees the right class, and a second unrelated family cannot see that child. Check a published activity, a correctly approved report and a sample fee record if those services are enabled. Complete the school-specific checks before bulk invitations.

Start here: your first school day — Move into the school day

My school day brings together real pupil/team/class counts, useful actions, remaining setup attention and fee information. Choose it as your home when ready; progress is not erased if setup is incomplete. Once all six checks are ready, the daily home becomes the default. Use More tools or sidebar search to find the wider toolkit. Existing core pages remain accessible without filling the first-day screen.

Start here: your first school day — Open your own account

Use the school sign-in link and your own email or school-issued sign-in ID. Families may receive a private one-time password-setup link instead of a temporary password; use that first. On first sign-in with a temporary credential, replace it with a private password of 12–72 characters. Check your name and school before working. A temporary handover normally expires after 72 hours; ask the school to verify your identity and issue a replacement if it expires. Never use a colleague’s login or send a password in chat.

Start here: your first school day — Understand your access

Your school activates the services it uses and assigns the responsibilities you need. One employee can hold several jobs on one login. Viewing, adding, approving and exporting are different permissions. A missing feature may need a reviewed assignment, class/branch scope, verified child link or active employment record; changing a job title alone does not grant access. Ask the School Admin to review the relevant connection instead of sharing another person’s account.

Start here: your first school day — Check your work and get help

Read the saved confirmation, then reopen the record to check the change. Notifications can be expanded or collapsed; browser sound starts after a permitted user interaction and may depend on device settings. Use School chat for permitted colleagues or the Help page for guidance. Report the page, time, action and visible error without including passwords, API keys, full identity documents or private pupil details in screenshots.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Begin with a calm home

Open Start my school for the six-step guide, My school day for everyday work, or More tools for a searchable toolkit. Existing records count towards readiness. Start with one teacher and one family, then grow. Optional services are not mandatory because they appear in the platform. Use Show me around to replay guidance; Listen to this step starts only when selected.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Save school details and branding

In Your school, save the school name, address, phone and email. If instructed, prepare installed workspaces; this is additive and preserves existing records. Use Logo, colours & document branding for the school logo, theme and document identity. The short school-details form preserves existing branding values. Use a valid supported image and the host’s protected logo-upload folder. Check a real report/receipt before sharing branded documents.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Set classes, subjects, sessions and teaching

In Your classes, follow the class/subject assistant. Use the school’s actual academic session names consistently. Add the real classes and subjects, then allocate teachers to the appropriate classes/subjects in the current session. Review term dates and calendar before sharing them. Before marks entry/publication, configure assessment components, score limits, weights, grade bands and the intended report format; readiness of class records alone does not validate assessment design.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Create staff without duplicate records

Open My team → Add a staff member. Enter name and optional email, select the main working dashboard and employment start date, verify identity and confirm whether employment is active for attendance. Under Already have a staff record?, select a matching existing HR/teacher record when applicable. Review the one-time sign-in handover and share it privately. Staff can also request access using the private school invitation; verify requests before approval.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Activate only needed jobs and services

Open Roles & permissions. Prepare it for this school if prompted, then choose the services used. Add/review templates or create a custom role. In People & access, select an employee, responsibilities, scope and financial approval ceiling. One employee may hold several responsibilities. New financial roles start at a zero approval ceiling. Assigning the first modular role deliberately adopts reviewed operational access; include all duties the employee still needs. Teaching allocations and personal employment access remain separate.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Use scopes honestly

Class/branch scopes are supported for pupil-linked leadership, examinations, accounts, support/security and review work where the record model supports them. Department scope applies to staff review work. Older aggregate HR, library, store and similar operational screens require whole-school grants. Unsupported scope combinations are refused. An individual permission block overrides role grants. Disabling a service retains its records and stops delegated use; School Admin setup/recovery access remains available.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Bring existing families and issue logins

Open Admissions → Family onboarding / existing families. Make the school registration link visible, generate/enable it and copy the school-specific link. Send it to existing parents through your chosen channel. Review the submitted parent details and every child; match school records and confirm the class before approval. Check delivery status. Platform SMTP can be used, or School Admin can configure school SMTP. WhatsApp handover opens a prepared message for review and Send; it is not an automatic background WhatsApp send.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Handle new admissions separately

Use the Admissions desk for enquiries, application review, entrance assessments/interviews where used, document checks and offers. Actual registration/admission creates or connects the pupil record and records class placement. Family/login setup connects authorised guardians and pupil logins. Keep duplicate checks and verification in place; a matching email/name alone is not authority over a child. Give an admissions employee a reviewed role rather than sharing the School Admin password.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Run fees with an audit trail

In Fees & Payments, create class fee plans, generate period invoices, record receipts and reconcile independently. A Principal can review balances and draft reminders without gaining money-processing authority. Configure the school gateway only if used, verify exact reference/currency/amount and test receipt/retry handling. Excess confirmed payment becomes credit; refunds and unpaid store-invoice withdrawal use their dedicated controls. Manual confirmation records funds independently verified elsewhere; it does not move money.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Support the Principal and follow up

New Principal templates include scoped fee view/export and follow-up drafts. Existing real-school roles are not silently expanded. In More tools → Principal financial reports, explicitly review the role before enabling new fee permissions; the change affects everyone assigned that role and retains existing scope. Fee, attendance, learning, teacher-support, staff-setup and family-login-help actions can prepare editable in-app chat drafts. Review the recipient and wording, then press Send.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Check school operations

Enable only transport, boarding, HR, library, store or other services the school runs. Connect drivers to active routes/pupils, wardens to duties, staff to active employment, and librarians/store officers to their working roles. Review the child/employee record after an operational action. Do not mistake a dashboard assignment for verified driving credentials, guardian collection permission, stock receipt, salary approval or a completed handover.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Protect records and recover access

Review parent links, staff duties, service switches and inactive leavers regularly. Recovery requires identity verification, a fresh temporary credential and first-sign-in password change; resetting a password invalidates previous sessions on their next request. School-owner recovery needs the platform team. Maintain formal privacy/retention/consent policies and encrypted-key/database backups. Use the audit history; do not delete accounting history to correct a mistake.

School Admin: set up and run your school — Prepare the first live school day

Test a teacher, parent, pupil and every enabled staff function on the actual host. Confirm HTTPS/private-file denial, a restorable backup, email delivery if used, report publication, fee reconciliation and target-size classroom media. Assign an owner for daily support and failed-delivery review. Launch a small supervised group first, monitor it and expand only after the school’s critical flows are confirmed.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Keep platform and school duties separate

Use the real Platform Super Admin login for host management, plans, integrations, demo maintenance and updates. School Admins control their own school records. The platform demo cannot manage the real host. Limit privileged access to authorised operators and use distinct private credentials. The platform does not provide in-app MFA in this edition; review host/access-layer protections with the hosting operator.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Check the supported runtime

Open Updates & Plugins → Before your first live school for read-only detected facts and outstanding live checks. Use a maintained PHP release; this code requires PHP 8.1 or newer and is locally exercised on PHP 8.4. Verify PDO MySQL, mbstring, OpenSSL, cURL, ZIP and fileinfo, database charset/collation compatibility, writable private storage and upload/time limits. A cPanel/PHP built-in test server is not a production server. Configure HTTPS and actual private-path denial on the deployed Apache/LiteSpeed/Nginx stack.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Back up before enabling schools

Keep database, application, uploads and private encryption keys together in a recoverable backup outside the public document root. Do not rotate/delete the private key while saved ciphertext depends on it. Test a restore to a separate environment and record who can perform recovery and expected recovery times. A file rollback is not a database rollback. Host backups/cron are operational responsibilities, not automatically proven by an application health screen.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Protect deployment files

Open Updates & Plugins → Protect private deployment artifacts on Apache where applicable, then verify unauthenticated denial from outside the server. Move installation/update ZIPs, database dumps, logs, old replacement files and test tools outside public_html. Nginx needs equivalent host rules. File presence of .htaccess is not evidence the host honours it. Remove/deny the installer after installation; the existing-config/lock guard must reject reinstallation.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Configure plans and registration

Set active plans, monthly/termly/annual prices and trials in SaaS controls. Configure the canonical HTTPS installation URL, signup policy and platform subscription gateway in the relevant platform settings. Test a school registration and exact server-verified subscription payment in test mode. Trial/expiry and administrative suspension are distinct. Renewals use an explicit checkout; do not promise automatic recurring bank debits.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Configure email and external services deliberately

Set shared SMTP for family-login delivery and test it. A school may select its own supported SMTP; blank secret fields preserve saved values. Other communication providers, credentials, consent, templates and credits are separate where configured. The family SMTP flow and provider-based communications do not automatically share every setting. Use the visible delivery result and provider logs; queued, available and delivered/read are different states.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Connect the shared live media server

In platform Setup & account, choose Shared server for every school when operating a central LiveKit-compatible service. Map Websocket URL to WSS origin, API key to API key, and the private API secret to API secret. Never put account email/password in those fields or publish the secret. Test the API check and an actual multi-device lesson. Cloud free tiers have quotas; self-hosting still has hosting/bandwidth costs. There is no guarantee of unlimited free stable participants.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Keep the isolated demo safe

Provision only an empty, separate database ending _demo with dedicated credentials. Demo health checks verify expected ownership. Prepare/upgrade existing fixed demo accounts without clearing them where possible. Refresh is a deliberate destructive reset of the marked fictional sandbox, not the production database. The 41 demo identities cover general, family and specialist accounts. No real child/family details or live provider keys should be entered there.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Review platform reports

Open Platform → Reports for actual school and active-login counts, identity totals, stored subscription-status history and verified platform checkout totals. Subscription history is not a count of currently paying schools; platform checkout totals exclude school fees and do not prove bank settlement/profit. Open SaaS controls for the underlying plan/subscription review and Updates & Plugins for operator launch checks.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Run and monitor workers

Use the installed dedicated CLI worker files only for features enabled by the school. Review each file’s CLI requirement and help/options; keep database/secret files private. Replace placeholders with actual absolute paths and school IDs. Examples: php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --hr-attendance --all-schools (calendar-day close); php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --tenant=123 --family-onboarding --limit=30 (family handovers); php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --tenant=123 --limit=30 (service deliveries); php /absolute/path/intelligence_worker.php 123 20 (scheduled intelligence work); php /absolute/path/affiliate_worker.php (affiliate reconciliation). php /absolute/path/experience_demo_refresh.php --confirm-isolated-demo deliberately resets only the marked separate demo; run it only in an approved quiet window. Some due work also processes from pages; regular unattended execution needs an operator-configured cron. Monitor failed jobs and provider responses; cron configuration is not created merely by saving a schedule.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Install trusted cumulative updates

Back up first. In Updates & Plugins, upload the exact trusted release ZIP without extracting or nesting it. Verify the published checksum, confirm the source and install. The updater checks approved destinations and file hashes, preserves configuration and journals recoverable changes. Packages are not digitally signed in this edition. Declarative plugin installation stores supported metadata; it does not execute arbitrary PHP plugins or create a working module automatically.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Recover a release consistently

After modular access adoption, incompatible file-only downgrade below 5.4.1 is refused. A compatible file rollback can restore files/metadata but cannot undo account, permission or financial changes. For a deeper recovery, use the matching database/key/file backup under a reviewed plan. Never repeatedly retry against a partially recovered database or delete access tables to bypass a safeguard.

Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Operate the launch as a service

Review errors, backups, account-link exceptions, failed mail, pending finance reconciliation, demo health and classroom provider quotas. Keep public policies/contact/support channels accurate. Restrict services that have not passed live acceptance. Distinguish code checks from proven SMTP/payment/media/restore behaviour on your host. Record incident ownership and a rollback/support window before onboarding the first real schools.

Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Choose the right intake route

Use the Admissions desk for enquiries/application review, offers and pupil admission. Existing-school families use Family onboarding / existing families. Do not recreate every existing child as a new applicant. The School Admin can grant admissions responsibilities to an employee through modular permissions; the same employee may have other reviewed duties.

Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Work enquiries and applications

Record accurate pupil/guardian details and the school’s actual class, then move the record through the relevant review stages. Enquiries can be followed up and converted. Review required documents, assessments/interviews and payments where the school uses them. Entrance assessment links are private bearer links; opening preparation should not by itself start the timer. Do not share links publicly.

Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Complete registration and admission

Use the registration record for the pupil’s required details, class and guardian. Review uploaded documents and valid replacements. Confirm the admission decision and any required verification; actual admission creates/connects the pupil and retains the source record.
