# What changed in this edition

- Replaced the flat ₦20,000/month ARPA scenario with progressive pupil pricing and an explicit school-size mix.
- Updated the business model: minimum term charge, one-month trial, annual upfront savings and prorated capacity/pupil upgrades.
- Added a transparent 500-pupil pricing walkthrough and an editable pupil-price calculator.
- Added the bounded founding-school offer and first-12-paid-month affiliate commission policy; existing-referral terms are not assumed to be retroactively rewritten.
- Separated core subscriptions, provider-funded usage and optional assisted onboarding. Setup fees are not counted as an automatically collected revenue stream.
- Built cost-aware first-paid-year cohort economics, a retained-cohort renewal case and scale sensitivity in the workbook. Delivery costs vary by cohort size; no unsupported gross-margin assumption is presented as fact.
- Added a current fictional pricing-page view alongside the school dashboard. Screenshots are product evidence, not traction.
- Expanded appendices from four to six while keeping 14 main slides and a 10-slide competition version.
- Updated presenter notes, application copy, sources and the financial workbook together. Both interactive scenarios and spreadsheet formula results are checked against the same reference model.

No new verified traction or funding ask has been supplied. The deck continues to say pilot launch/recruitment, with all school-count, retention, cost and budget assumptions labeled.
