Commercial example: 500 pupils cost ₦232,500 per four-month term, ₦58,125 monthly or ₦627,750 annual upfront at the reference 10% annual saving. A qualifying 20%-off founding annual example is ₦502,200, with ₦100,440 first-year commission on an eligible new referral at 20%, before other costs. Founding qualification/availability must be confirmed in-app.

Cohort model: assumed school-size mix, all annual-paid subscriptions, bounded founding discount, partner-originated share, first-12-paid-month commission and size-specific delivery costs. Contribution is after modeled variable costs, before fixed overhead, tax and incremental acquisition/implementation spending. It is not actual profit. School tuition collections, separately funded provider usage and optional setup fees are excluded from subscription revenue. The retained-cohort year-2 case assumes 80% retention and no new schools; this is not measured retention.

Open Sans fonts are bundled under their license. Native PowerPoint remains editable; the HTML embeds fonts and screenshots. Product screenshots contain fictional records.
