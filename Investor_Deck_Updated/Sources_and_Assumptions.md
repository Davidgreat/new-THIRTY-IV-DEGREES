Weighted standard annual subscription: ₦477,225 per school. Fractional cohort allocations are scenario weights, not actual headcounts.

At that mix, a modeled 20-place founding offer saves ₦1,006,200 against standard annual subscriptions of ₦119,306,250. After-offer subscriptions are ₦118,300,050. Assume 40% new partner-originated schools, 20% first-paid-year commission and uniform referral share across discounted/regular collections: commission expense ₦9,464,004. A 2% effective payment expense is a modeling assumption, not a Paystack tariff or verified blended cost.

Monthly core hosting/support delivery-cost assumptions by cohort: ₦5,000 / ₦10,000 / ₦20,000 / ₦40,000 per school. Total annual direct cost at the default mix: ₦34,500,000. Contribution after those variable costs, referral commission and payment cost: ₦71,970,045 (60.8%). This is not actual profit or a forecast. Optional fixed overhead is modeled separately at ₦1.5m/month, leaving ₦53,970,045 before tax and incremental acquisition/implementation costs under those assumptions. No actual CAC payback or LTV is claimed.

The retained-cohort year-2 workbook case assumes 80% retention, the same school-size mix and no newly acquired schools. The founding year and new referral allowance have ended for that retained annual-paid cohort; historical or new-referral obligations cannot be ignored in a real business forecast. No actual retention has been measured. Annual sums / 12 are monthly equivalents, not accounting recognition or a cash-flow schedule.

School tuition collected for a school is not SchoolOS subscription revenue. Provider-funded SMS/AI/media/storage usage is not counted as platform subscription income. Optional assisted setup remains a quote/helpdesk/payment-arrangement workflow; no actual setup revenue, automatic service charging or metering is claimed.
