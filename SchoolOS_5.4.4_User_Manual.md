# SchoolOS 5.4.4 — Complete SchoolOS user manual

## About this manual

SchoolOS Nigeria 5.4.4. Updated 17 November 2026 (WAT). This manual describes current implemented workflows, role boundaries and provider-dependent services. Menus vary with school services, assignments and record links. Read the short Start here guide first; use role chapters as needed. There are 35 staff job profiles and 41 demo identities. Production schools activate only the jobs they need. No guide grants access or replaces school policy, safeguarding, accounting or legal review.

## Start here: your first school day — Know the three kinds of access

The Platform Super Admin operates SchoolOS for participating schools. A School Admin sets up and controls one school. Staff, learners and parents work in their own school accounts. A job profile is a responsibility, not a separate school or a universal administrator. A school with five employees can combine suitable responsibilities on existing logins; a larger school can split duties and scopes. Activate only the services the school actually operates.

## Start here: your first school day — Start a new school

Use Start your school. Enter the school and owner details, choose an available plan and billing cycle, and review the configured price/trial. Sign in to the created owner account and complete any required subscription step. Prices, trials and availability come from platform settings; they are not identical for every school. A payment-return page alone is not verification. An administratively suspended school needs platform review even after paying.

## Start here: your first school day — Use Start my school

The School Admin home has Start my school, My school day and More tools. Start my school shows six saved-record checks: Your school, Your classes, Your teaching team, Safe access, Your pupils and Connect families. Pick the highlighted next step and follow the short instruction. The percentage updates from actual records. Listen to this step is optional browser/device voice with a Stop control; no pupil names or balances are read aloud.

## Start here: your first school day — Know what can wait

Open What can wait? for fees, assessments, dates/calendar, email delivery, extra jobs/services and school policy work. These do not reduce the six-step percentage, but must be configured before you rely on the related service. Review privacy and retention before wider pupil-data use. A saved access-plan acknowledgement records the owner’s plan; it is not legal certification or approval of every formal school policy.

## Start here: your first school day — Bring an existing school on board

Create current classes and sessions first. Use Admissions → existing families / Family onboarding for the school-specific parent registration link. A parent supplies their own contact details and one or more children. The school reviews each child against existing records, matches an existing pupil or approves a new record, and confirms the class. Manual family entry is also available. Do not process an existing enrolled child as a fresh applicant just to obtain a login.

## Start here: your first school day — Bring a new pupil into the school

Use Admissions for an enquiry or application where needed, and the pupil registration/admission workflow for actual intake. Review required details/documents, fees if the school requires them, class placement and the admission decision. Approval creates or connects the pupil record. Family & login setup then creates or connects verified parent/learner access. An unreviewed public form never creates unrestricted access to children’s records.

## Start here: your first school day — Connect the first teacher

Open My team and add a staff member. Select Teacher, verify identity and employment, and connect an existing HR/teacher record when one already exists. Give the teacher their current-session classes and subjects in Academics. Hand over the temporary sign-in privately. Test that teacher’s view before inviting the whole team. An HR record, directory entry and login are connected records with different purposes.

## Start here: your first school day — Test one complete family

Approve one parent and all their verified child links. Share each person’s own sign-in. Check the parent sees only their children, the pupil sees the right class, and a second unrelated family cannot see that child. Check a published activity, a correctly approved report and a sample fee record if those services are enabled. Complete the school-specific checks before bulk invitations.

## Start here: your first school day — Move into the school day

My school day brings together real pupil/team/class counts, useful actions, remaining setup attention and fee information. Choose it as your home when ready; progress is not erased if setup is incomplete. Once all six checks are ready, the daily home becomes the default. Use More tools or sidebar search to find the wider toolkit. Existing core pages remain accessible without filling the first-day screen.

## Start here: your first school day — Open your own account

Use the school sign-in link and your own email or school-issued sign-in ID. Families may receive a private one-time password-setup link instead of a temporary password; use that first. On first sign-in with a temporary credential, replace it with a private password of 12–72 characters. Check your name and school before working. A temporary handover normally expires after 72 hours; ask the school to verify your identity and issue a replacement if it expires. Never use a colleague’s login or send a password in chat.

## Start here: your first school day — Understand your access

Your school activates the services it uses and assigns the responsibilities you need. One employee can hold several jobs on one login. Viewing, adding, approving and exporting are different permissions. A missing feature may need a reviewed assignment, class/branch scope, verified child link or active employment record; changing a job title alone does not grant access. Ask the School Admin to review the relevant connection instead of sharing another person’s account.

## Start here: your first school day — Check your work and get help

Read the saved confirmation, then reopen the record to check the change. Notifications can be expanded or collapsed; browser sound starts after a permitted user interaction and may depend on device settings. Use School chat for permitted colleagues or the Help page for guidance. Report the page, time, action and visible error without including passwords, API keys, full identity documents or private pupil details in screenshots.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Begin with a calm home

Open Start my school for the six-step guide, My school day for everyday work, or More tools for a searchable toolkit. Existing records count towards readiness. Start with one teacher and one family, then grow. Optional services are not mandatory because they appear in the platform. Use Show me around to replay guidance; Listen to this step starts only when selected.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Save school details and branding

In Your school, save the school name, address, phone and email. If instructed, prepare installed workspaces; this is additive and preserves existing records. Use Logo, colours & document branding for the school logo, theme and document identity. The short school-details form preserves existing branding values. Use a valid supported image and the host’s protected logo-upload folder. Check a real report/receipt before sharing branded documents.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Set classes, subjects, sessions and teaching

In Your classes, follow the class/subject assistant. Use the school’s actual academic session names consistently. Add the real classes and subjects, then allocate teachers to the appropriate classes/subjects in the current session. Review term dates and calendar before sharing them. Before marks entry/publication, configure assessment components, score limits, weights, grade bands and the intended report format; readiness of class records alone does not validate assessment design.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Create staff without duplicate records

Open My team → Add a staff member. Enter name and optional email, select the main working dashboard and employment start date, verify identity and confirm whether employment is active for attendance. Under Already have a staff record?, select a matching existing HR/teacher record when applicable. Review the one-time sign-in handover and share it privately. Staff can also request access using the private school invitation; verify requests before approval.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Activate only needed jobs and services

Open Roles & permissions. Prepare it for this school if prompted, then choose the services used. Add/review templates or create a custom role. In People & access, select an employee, responsibilities, scope and financial approval ceiling. One employee may hold several responsibilities. New financial roles start at a zero approval ceiling. Assigning the first modular role deliberately adopts reviewed operational access; include all duties the employee still needs. Teaching allocations and personal employment access remain separate.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Use scopes honestly

Class/branch scopes are supported for pupil-linked leadership, examinations, accounts, support/security and review work where the record model supports them. Department scope applies to staff review work. Older aggregate HR, library, store and similar operational screens require whole-school grants. Unsupported scope combinations are refused. An individual permission block overrides role grants. Disabling a service retains its records and stops delegated use; School Admin setup/recovery access remains available.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Bring existing families and issue logins

Open Admissions → Family onboarding / existing families. Make the school registration link visible, generate/enable it and copy the school-specific link. Send it to existing parents through your chosen channel. Review the submitted parent details and every child; match school records and confirm the class before approval. Check delivery status. Platform SMTP can be used, or School Admin can configure school SMTP. WhatsApp handover opens a prepared message for review and Send; it is not an automatic background WhatsApp send.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Handle new admissions separately

Use the Admissions desk for enquiries, application review, entrance assessments/interviews where used, document checks and offers. Actual registration/admission creates or connects the pupil record and records class placement. Family/login setup connects authorised guardians and pupil logins. Keep duplicate checks and verification in place; a matching email/name alone is not authority over a child. Give an admissions employee a reviewed role rather than sharing the School Admin password.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Run fees with an audit trail

In Fees & Payments, create class fee plans, generate period invoices, record receipts and reconcile independently. A Principal can review balances and draft reminders without gaining money-processing authority. Configure the school gateway only if used, verify exact reference/currency/amount and test receipt/retry handling. Excess confirmed payment becomes credit; refunds and unpaid store-invoice withdrawal use their dedicated controls. Manual confirmation records funds independently verified elsewhere; it does not move money.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Support the Principal and follow up

New Principal templates include scoped fee view/export and follow-up drafts. Existing real-school roles are not silently expanded. In More tools → Principal financial reports, explicitly review the role before enabling new fee permissions; the change affects everyone assigned that role and retains existing scope. Fee, attendance, learning, teacher-support, staff-setup and family-login-help actions can prepare editable in-app chat drafts. Review the recipient and wording, then press Send.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Check school operations

Enable only transport, boarding, HR, library, store or other services the school runs. Connect drivers to active routes/pupils, wardens to duties, staff to active employment, and librarians/store officers to their working roles. Review the child/employee record after an operational action. Do not mistake a dashboard assignment for verified driving credentials, guardian collection permission, stock receipt, salary approval or a completed handover.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Protect records and recover access

Review parent links, staff duties, service switches and inactive leavers regularly. Recovery requires identity verification, a fresh temporary credential and first-sign-in password change; resetting a password invalidates previous sessions on their next request. School-owner recovery needs the platform team. Maintain formal privacy/retention/consent policies and encrypted-key/database backups. Use the audit history; do not delete accounting history to correct a mistake.

## School Admin: set up and run your school — Prepare the first live school day

Test a teacher, parent, pupil and every enabled staff function on the actual host. Confirm HTTPS/private-file denial, a restorable backup, email delivery if used, report publication, fee reconciliation and target-size classroom media. Assign an owner for daily support and failed-delivery review. Launch a small supervised group first, monitor it and expand only after the school’s critical flows are confirmed.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Keep platform and school duties separate

Use the real Platform Super Admin login for host management, plans, integrations, demo maintenance and updates. School Admins control their own school records. The platform demo cannot manage the real host. Limit privileged access to authorised operators and use distinct private credentials. The platform does not provide in-app MFA in this edition; review host/access-layer protections with the hosting operator.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Check the supported runtime

Open Updates & Plugins → Before your first live school for read-only detected facts and outstanding live checks. Use a maintained PHP release; this code requires PHP 8.1 or newer and is locally exercised on PHP 8.4. Verify PDO MySQL, mbstring, OpenSSL, cURL, ZIP and fileinfo, database charset/collation compatibility, writable private storage and upload/time limits. A cPanel/PHP built-in test server is not a production server. Configure HTTPS and actual private-path denial on the deployed Apache/LiteSpeed/Nginx stack.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Back up before enabling schools

Keep database, application, uploads and private encryption keys together in a recoverable backup outside the public document root. Do not rotate/delete the private key while saved ciphertext depends on it. Test a restore to a separate environment and record who can perform recovery and expected recovery times. A file rollback is not a database rollback. Host backups/cron are operational responsibilities, not automatically proven by an application health screen.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Protect deployment files

Open Updates & Plugins → Protect private deployment artifacts on Apache where applicable, then verify unauthenticated denial from outside the server. Move installation/update ZIPs, database dumps, logs, old replacement files and test tools outside public_html. Nginx needs equivalent host rules. File presence of .htaccess is not evidence the host honours it. Remove/deny the installer after installation; the existing-config/lock guard must reject reinstallation.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Configure plans and registration

Set active plans, monthly/termly/annual prices and trials in SaaS controls. Configure the canonical HTTPS installation URL, signup policy and platform subscription gateway in the relevant platform settings. Test a school registration and exact server-verified subscription payment in test mode. Trial/expiry and administrative suspension are distinct. Renewals use an explicit checkout; do not promise automatic recurring bank debits.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Configure email and external services deliberately

Set shared SMTP for family-login delivery and test it. A school may select its own supported SMTP; blank secret fields preserve saved values. Other communication providers, credentials, consent, templates and credits are separate where configured. The family SMTP flow and provider-based communications do not automatically share every setting. Use the visible delivery result and provider logs; queued, available and delivered/read are different states.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Connect the shared live media server

In platform Setup & account, choose Shared server for every school when operating a central LiveKit-compatible service. Map Websocket URL to WSS origin, API key to API key, and the private API secret to API secret. Never put account email/password in those fields or publish the secret. Test the API check and an actual multi-device lesson. Cloud free tiers have quotas; self-hosting still has hosting/bandwidth costs. There is no guarantee of unlimited free stable participants.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Keep the isolated demo safe

Provision only an empty, separate database ending _demo with dedicated credentials. Demo health checks verify expected ownership. Prepare/upgrade existing fixed demo accounts without clearing them where possible. Refresh is a deliberate destructive reset of the marked fictional sandbox, not the production database. The 41 demo identities cover general, family and specialist accounts. No real child/family details or live provider keys should be entered there.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Review platform reports

Open Platform → Reports for actual school and active-login counts, identity totals, stored subscription-status history and verified platform checkout totals. Subscription history is not a count of currently paying schools; platform checkout totals exclude school fees and do not prove bank settlement/profit. Open SaaS controls for the underlying plan/subscription review and Updates & Plugins for operator launch checks.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Run and monitor workers

Use the installed dedicated CLI worker files only for features enabled by the school. Review each file’s CLI requirement and help/options; keep database/secret files private. Replace placeholders with actual absolute paths and school IDs. Examples: php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --hr-attendance --all-schools (calendar-day close); php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --tenant=123 --family-onboarding --limit=30 (family handovers); php /absolute/path/services_worker.php --tenant=123 --limit=30 (service deliveries); php /absolute/path/intelligence_worker.php 123 20 (scheduled intelligence work); php /absolute/path/affiliate_worker.php (affiliate reconciliation). php /absolute/path/experience_demo_refresh.php --confirm-isolated-demo deliberately resets only the marked separate demo; run it only in an approved quiet window. Some due work also processes from pages; regular unattended execution needs an operator-configured cron. Monitor failed jobs and provider responses; cron configuration is not created merely by saving a schedule.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Install trusted cumulative updates

Back up first. In Updates & Plugins, upload the exact trusted release ZIP without extracting or nesting it. Verify the published checksum, confirm the source and install. The updater checks approved destinations and file hashes, preserves configuration and journals recoverable changes. Packages are not digitally signed in this edition. Declarative plugin installation stores supported metadata; it does not execute arbitrary PHP plugins or create a working module automatically.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Recover a release consistently

After modular access adoption, incompatible file-only downgrade below 5.4.1 is refused. A compatible file rollback can restore files/metadata but cannot undo account, permission or financial changes. For a deeper recovery, use the matching database/key/file backup under a reviewed plan. Never repeatedly retry against a partially recovered database or delete access tables to bypass a safeguard.

## Platform Super Admin: operate SchoolOS — Operate the launch as a service

Review errors, backups, account-link exceptions, failed mail, pending finance reconciliation, demo health and classroom provider quotas. Keep public policies/contact/support channels accurate. Restrict services that have not passed live acceptance. Distinguish code checks from proven SMTP/payment/media/restore behaviour on your host. Record incident ownership and a rollback/support window before onboarding the first real schools.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Choose the right intake route

Use the Admissions desk for enquiries/application review, offers and pupil admission. Existing-school families use Family onboarding / existing families. Do not recreate every existing child as a new applicant. The School Admin can grant admissions responsibilities to an employee through modular permissions; the same employee may have other reviewed duties.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Work enquiries and applications

Record accurate pupil/guardian details and the school’s actual class, then move the record through the relevant review stages. Enquiries can be followed up and converted. Review required documents, assessments/interviews and payments where the school uses them. Entrance assessment links are private bearer links; opening preparation should not by itself start the timer. Do not share links publicly.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Complete registration and admission

Use the registration record for the pupil’s required details, class and guardian. Review uploaded documents and valid replacements. Confirm the admission decision and any required verification; actual admission creates/connects the pupil and retains the source record. Bulk imports must be validated with correct headers/classes and checked for duplicates. Import a small sample before a whole cohort.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Share the existing-family school link

The School Admin and authorised Admissions desk expose Family onboarding and the school-specific share controls. Check branding and copy the enabled link. The shared demo exposes only a labelled fictional preview, not a production registration link. If the live link is absent, ask the School Admin to prepare onboarding/canonical origin rather than sharing an empty box or the demo preview as a real school link.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Review each family and child

Read the parent email/WhatsApp and relationship. For each child, compare school identity/records, match an existing pupil or confirm a new one, select the right class and review authority. More than one child can be approved; partial approval/corrections remain visible. Return an error for correction rather than approving a near match. A parent account must not acquire another child based only on an email or name.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Hand over connected accounts

After verified approval, the family workflow creates/connects parent and requested pupil logins, links records and issues sign-in IDs and one-time password-setup links. Setup links expire in 72 hours; existing users retain personal passwords. Check delivery history and use the available reissue/resend action where needed. Email depends on configured platform/school SMTP. WhatsApp opens a prepared message for explicit review and Send; it is not an automatic API delivery. Share credentials only through the intended verified channel. SMTP settings are School Admin controlled.

## Admissions officer: new intake and existing families — Stay within admissions authority

A completed admission is not an assessment publication, fee reconciliation, transport assignment or staff account approval. Follow the linked next-step controls for those duties. Use your own attendance/employment view for clock-in. Ask the School Admin about missing roles; do not create a generic staff login for a parent/learner to bypass family review.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Check assigned classes first

Open your teaching overview/studio and confirm the current classes and subjects. Teacher access follows the verified login and current-session allocations. If a class is missing, ask the School Admin or authorised coordinator to check your allocation; a teacher directory name alone does not establish access. Personal staff attendance is available through employment controls when your active employee record is linked.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Plan and teach

Use the teaching pages for timetable, lesson plans, syllabus progress and resources. Work in the correct class/subject/session. Save the actual lesson topic/content and only mark lessons delivered when appropriate. Published learning courses and released activities become visible to the linked class/family according to their visibility controls; a saved draft does not automatically become homework.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Mark pupil attendance

Select the correct class/date, record present/late/absent with an appropriate reason, save and review the register. A missing pupil entry is unmarked, not proof of absence. Staff clock-in is separate from pupil attendance. Correct a mistake through the authorised attendance workflow rather than changing another pupil or using another teacher’s login.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Share work and give feedback

Create/select the course and activity, check availability/due dates and publish the course/work. Learners submit using their own accounts. Open the same submission to mark it and save feedback. Approved/published progress follows the existing family view. Attach only supported file types within the form’s size limit. Private attachments are downloaded through permission checks, not public shared folders.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Record marks and release reports

Use the school’s configured assessment components, raw maxima and weights. Validate any score import and inspect its preview before confirmation. Submit marks for independent review. A term report needs approved complete components, valid weights and school publication; parents do not see drafts just because a teacher saved a score. Ask the authorised examinations team to resolve missing assessments or incorrect report format instead of inventing grades.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Schedule a lesson in half-hour steps

In Live classroom, create the lesson for the allocated class/subject, choose date/start time and a duration from the available 30-minute increments. The class card has its scheduled time/countdown. Use the configured inbuilt media service or a supported external meeting invitation. Shared media configuration is managed by the platform policy, not by entering a teacher’s own cloud password.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Start, share and manage the room

When you start, waiting learners automatically connect. Use the distinct screen-sharing control to share or stop sharing; End class is a separate action. Keep room chat/questions/tests within the lesson controls. Learners can react/raise hands; select an appropriate learner to speak or open responses to all, using the available speaking controls. Microphone permission and browser/device rules still apply; selecting a learner cannot override the device’s privacy permission.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Pause, end and clear lessons

Pause a recoverable session when appropriate. Resume/reactivate within the valid scheduled window if that control is available. Permanent ending closes the live session; completed cards can be archived/hidden through the appropriate role controls. Do not leave a class permanently ended and ask learners to keep rejoining. Check what the room says if the host disconnects; a lost network is different from an intentionally ended lesson.

## Teacher: teaching, records and live classes — Keep teaching and family contact connected

School chat supports permitted family/learner relationships, emoji and message reactions. Typing status is temporary presence while someone is actively entering a message, not delivery or read proof. Principal/academic follow-ups can open a draft conversation; review it before Send. Keep sensitive welfare cases in the separately authorised support workflow rather than putting confidential notes in a class group.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Join an existing school

Use the exact school-issued registration link and check the school name/branding. Enter your own full name, email, WhatsApp number and authorised relationship. Add each child separately and supply accurate school/class/identity details. Submit once and wait for school review. This is for the school to match its records; submitting a form does not itself enrol a new child or give unrestricted access. Ask the school about changes rather than submitting conflicting duplicates.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Receive and protect family sign-ins

After verified approval the school creates/connects the parent and any requested pupil logins. New family accounts receive one-time password-setup links valid for 72 hours; the parent may help a young child choose their own password. Existing connected users keep their personal passwords. A child without a separate requested login can still be available through the verified parent account. Email uses the configured shared or school SMTP; the school can also prepare a WhatsApp handover and explicitly send it. Use each person’s own sign-in and change temporary passwords when prompted. If mail is missing, check junk and ask the school to inspect delivery/reissue. Never send passwords back in School chat.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Select the correct child

The family home lists the children you are verified to access. Choose a child before attendance, homework, fees, reports, transport or requests. Check the child’s name in each view. A missing child or incorrect class needs school verification; a matching family surname/email is not enough. Parents can chat with their linked children and other permitted school contacts.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Follow learning and print reports

Use learning/homework for published/released work and your child’s feedback. Open Results for detailed approved marks. A printable digital report needs the school’s published term and assigned report format; it uses school identity, configured blocks/grades, comments and available attendance/resumption information. Choose the intended child/session/term, open Print and save as PDF or print. Missing setup/completeness messages must be resolved by the school; parents cannot publish or alter marks.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Review fees and receipts

Open the selected child’s fees, check invoice reference, description, balance and due date. Use the offered school payment option and keep the receipt/reference. Bank/card success alone is not a reconciled school ledger; report a delay to accounts with the reference, without sharing card secrets. Family access does not grant finance approval. Refunds/credits follow the school’s controlled accounts process.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Manage absence, permissions and appointments

Submit absence/permission requests with correct dates and reasons. Pending is not approval. Review replies and school events. Use available booking/appointment choices for meetings. Keep contact details accurate and complete requested consents only when you understand the purpose. School messages, announcement availability, read acknowledgement and external delivery are different things.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Use the school bus safely

Review your child’s assigned route and live-updating journey record. Pickup, not travelling, home boarding and completed drop-off are separate outcomes. Set up an approved regular collector for the allowed date range, or a today-only change when someone different will collect. A valid regular approval avoids a daily repeat request; a today-only change takes precedence and requires school review. Revoke an authorisation when circumstances change. Supply a one-time code if required by school policy; drivers still verify identity.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Read and shop without staff administration

The family library is for your children’s permitted catalogue/resources, requests/reservations, current loans/due dates, overdue information and reading history. It does not grant catalogue editing or procurement. In the school shop, view item photos and the correct available size/colour/variant, choose child/quantity, submit the request and follow invoice/payment/collection status. Requesting does not mean stock has been collected; the store records fulfilment.

## Parent & guardian: your children, together — Use chat and live lessons

Chat with your verified children, assigned teachers/driver or permitted school office. Emoji and reactions express a response; typing indicates temporary activity only. Live lessons appear for the correct child/class. If the teacher has not started, the waiting room explains this and connects when the class begins. Keep the lesson page open. Browser permission/network issues may need a refresh or school support; do not create another child account to work around them.

## Student: learning and your school day — Check your learning identity

Your login should resolve to one verified pupil record, the right class and current session. If the wrong name/class appears, stop and ask the school to correct the connection. Do not use another pupil’s login. The school’s student view determines which published learning, reports, notices and services you can access.

## Student: learning and your school day — Open work and submit it

In the learning area, open available class courses and activities, read the instructions and due date, complete the work and submit. Check the saved/submitted state. A draft answer is not necessarily final submission. Review teacher feedback through the same activity. If a graded submission is locked, request a correction through the teacher rather than creating duplicate work.

## Student: learning and your school day — Take a computer-based test

Open the assigned published examination/CBT, follow its start rules and timer, save answers as offered and submit once. Reopening an active attempt resumes the existing attempt when permitted; it does not guarantee a fresh timer. Final answers are marked on the server. Stable connectivity and the school’s exam policy matter; report a problem promptly rather than guessing that an offline screen saved successfully.

## Student: learning and your school day — Join a live lesson

Use the class card/countdown for the correct session. You can wait in the room before the teacher starts; the room connects when the teacher starts. Use room chat/questions/tests and reactions without leaving the lesson. Raise your hand to request speaking time. Follow the teacher’s speaking mode and microphone rules; only use your microphone when permitted and after granting device permission. Stop screen sharing only if you were the sharer; teacher sharing/end controls are distinct.

## Student: learning and your school day — Read reports and school updates

Marks/reports become available after school approval/publication, not as soon as a teacher enters a draft. Choose the right term and ask the school about a missing report. Expand notifications to read details and collapse them when finished. A live-class end/pause notification explains a room state; a permanently ended lesson cannot be entered as a new live session.

## Student: learning and your school day — Chat and use school services responsibly

Use permitted classmates, family and teachers. Other staff may send one-way route/library notices without being allowed a private student chat. Emojis, reactions and temporary typing presence are available in school chat. Library borrowing and school-shop requests follow school rules and stock availability. Your view does not expose procurement, payroll or other pupils’ private records.

## Principal / Head teacher — Open your leadership overview

Your leadership home separates My school view, Pupils & follow-ups, Fees & follow-ups and Teaching & academic actions. Counts and records follow the assigned school/class/branch scope. Open All my assigned responsibilities for any extra jobs you hold. Academic leadership does not automatically grant School Admin, payroll, confidential counselling or money approval.

## Principal / Head teacher — Check pupils and attendance

Search permitted pupils and inspect today’s register status. Unmarked means no entry yet, not proof of absence. Use the attendance/learning follow-up action for a verified parent. The wording is prepared from the relevant record; check it before Send. If no permitted parent is offered, ask the School Admin to verify the family connection and communication permission.

## Principal / Head teacher — Support teaching and review publication

In Teaching & academic actions, review class teachers, current-session allocations and published-result status. Use academic action notes to record a clear task, responsible colleague and progress. Teacher-support chat drafts require an allocation within both your academic and follow-up scope. Confidential counselling belongs to its separately authorised workspace.

## Principal / Head teacher — See fees without becoming a payment approver

With Leadership fee reports permission, see billed/collected/remaining values, owing pupils, due dates and invoice records. Search/filter by name, reference or permitted class. Export needs a separate permission. Invoice allocations are not bank balance/profit. Verify the requesting person’s identity and authority, select the correct existing request/record and record a proportionate decision/action with evidence. Protect other pupils/parents/staff when producing an export or response. A name/email match alone is not authority to disclose a child’s data.

## School privacy officer — Review access and retention

Work with the School Admin to remove unnecessary roles, revoke expired sharing/collector links and apply the school’s retention process. Finance, review and incident audit history may have separate retention needs. Back up private keys with records, restrict exported files and document incidents through the approved school process.

## General school staff & personal employment — Open your own account

Use the school sign-in link and your own email or school-issued sign-in ID. Families may receive a private one-time password-setup link instead of a temporary password; use that first. On first sign-in with a temporary credential, replace it with a private password of 12–72 characters. Check your name and school before working. A temporary handover normally expires after 72 hours; ask the school to verify your identity and issue a replacement if it expires. Never use a colleague’s login or send a password in chat.

## General school staff & personal employment — Find the work assigned to you

Your general staff account gives personal employment and permitted school communications; specialist work appears only when assigned. Use All my assigned responsibilities if you hold more than one job. General personal tasks are notes/work reminders; they are not pupil transport outcomes, library returns or official stock transactions.

## General school staff & personal employment — Clock in and manage your employment

Use My attendance & employment or the dashboard attendance control. Check today’s WAT date and clock in on actual arrival, then clock out when leaving. An HR-linked active/exiting employment record is required; onboarding/future/unlinked records need HR review. Use your own leave request, query reply, payslip/development and other personal controls as available. You cannot clock for another employee or approve your own restricted request.

## General school staff & personal employment — Keep school communication appropriate

Staff can chat with permitted colleagues. Private learner contact is restricted to appropriate teaching/family relationships; a non-teaching job does not automatically grant it. Drivers/librarians may send permitted one-way service notifications. Use notifications and school chat for real school work, not a shared-password workaround.

## General school staff & personal employment — Understand your access

Your school activates the services it uses and assigns the responsibilities you need. One employee can hold several jobs on one login. Viewing, adding, approving and exporting are different permissions. A missing feature may need a reviewed assignment, class/branch scope, verified child link or active employment record; changing a job title alone does not grant access. Ask the School Admin to review the relevant connection instead of sharing another person’s account.

## General school staff & personal employment — Check your work and get help

Read the saved confirmation, then reopen the record to check the change. Notifications can be expanded or collapsed; browser sound starts after a permitted user interaction and may depend on device settings. Use School chat for permitted colleagues or the Help page for guidance. Report the page, time, action and visible error without including passwords, API keys, full identity documents or private pupil details in screenshots.

The affiliate/partner account is distinct from a school employee or pupil. Read the programme terms and the configured commission/hold/minimum withdrawal settings. Use your own referral link and truthful statements about features/pricing. An enquiry or visit is not a verified paid referral.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Set a bounded launch

Use a small supervised first school/cohort with named support owners. Enable services that have passed the relevant school-specific checks. Keep optional providers/large live classes disabled until tested. Source/regression tests provide evidence about exercised scenarios, not a blanket certificate for every host, dataset or concurrent workload.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Prove host and recovery basics

From a normal browser/network confirm HTTPS, secure sessions and actual denial of private storage/backups/config/installer artefacts. Confirm supported PHP/extensions and sufficient resources. Restore a matching database/files/uploads/private-key backup into a separate environment and record the outcome. An ordinary page returning 403 from an audit network does not prove each private rule works.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Prove identities and access

Test a School Admin, teacher, parent with multiple children, pupil and each activated staff job. Confirm verified child links, teacher allocations and employee/duty records. Check an unrelated family and another school cannot see the record. Check a revoked role, expired temporary sign-in and password reset stop access as expected. Resolve account-link warnings before broad invitations.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Prove the school’s daily workflows

Admit/match a sample pupil, approve a family, deliver/reissue login, mark attendance, publish/submit/mark work, independently publish a complete report, print it with branding, and check library/store/transport/boarding/HR functions actually used. A saved form should be checked from both staff and family views. Do not declare an untested optional function active just because its menu is visible.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Prove external services individually

Test real SMTP arrival/failure; provider messaging consent/delivery; gateway verification/reference/currency/amount/retry/receipt; and media across two networks at the intended class size. Browser permissions/quotas/network conditions matter. A mock provider test cannot confirm real settlement, mail arrival or LiveKit capacity. Monitor workers and delivery queues; configure cron explicitly where needed.

## Launch checklist and ongoing support — Operate and improve

Review errors, failed sends, pending reconciliation, missing attendance/relationships, backups, storage and provider quotas daily during launch. Keep a support route and quiet rollback window. Triage issues by impact, protect pupil information in reports and apply trusted cumulative updates with staging checks. Continue development without promising that every planned feature is already implemented.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — I cannot sign in

Check the school sign-in address, your own email or school-issued ID, account approval and temporary-credential expiry. Ask the school to verify identity and reissue if needed. A password reset invalidates older sessions on their next request. A suspension/subscription restriction is not solved by creating another account.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — My child, class or working page is missing

Parents need verified child links; students need exactly one learner connection/current class; teachers need current-session allocations; staff need the appropriate active role/service/duty. Employment and route/hostel assignments are separate. Ask the responsible administrator to review the actual record, not merely rename your job.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — A chat or notification is not behaving as expected

Check recipient permissions and the correct account on separate browsers/devices. Typing is temporary and stops after inactivity; it is not read/delivery proof. Refresh an expired session. Browser autoplay rules require interaction before sound; device volume/permissions can still block it. A collapsed notification counter can remain visible; open details to review/read messages.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — A live class is waiting or blank

Check the scheduled time, correct child/class, teacher’s started/paused/ended state and network connection. Waiting learners should connect when the teacher starts. Camera/mic/screen capture need HTTPS and browser permission. Shared SFU must be configured and have capacity; direct fallback has an eight-person total cap and greater teacher bandwidth use. Test target devices and networks. An ended lesson needs valid teacher reactivation or a newly scheduled session, not repeated blind joins.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — A payment is pending or the balance looks wrong

Keep the exact invoice/payment reference, amount and channel. Ask accounts to check server verification, independent reconciliation, credits/refunds and the correct child. Do not submit repeated cash confirmations or assume a payment-return screen posted the ledger. Only authorised accounts staff correct the source records.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — A report cannot print

Choose the correct child/session/term and check school publication. The school must resolve missing subjects/components, unapproved marks, invalid weights or missing assigned report format. A parent cannot fix this by editing marks. Use the approved school correction path for a published report.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — A drop-off, store order or library action is blocked

For bus drop-off, first mark the child on the home bus and check the current approved collector/identity/token. For a store order, check stock reservation, payment and collection/return state. For a library item, use the actual current loan/copy record. Do not fake another outcome to enable a button.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — An email or WhatsApp handover did not arrive

Ask the school to inspect its delivery record and verify the recipient contact. Saved SMTP settings are not a delivery test. Check spam/junk and expiry; reissue through the verified school workflow. A WhatsApp prepared-message link still needs the sender to open WhatsApp and press Send.

## Troubleshooting and common questions — I get a technical error

Record the page, time, last action and short error text; stop repeating a financial/approval action until its saved status is checked. Share a minimal redacted screenshot with the support team. Report mixed-collation/database errors to the operator; do not change database collation, delete tables or paste server secrets into chat.
