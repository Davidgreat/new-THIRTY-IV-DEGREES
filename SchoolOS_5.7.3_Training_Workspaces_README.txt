SchoolOS 5.7.3 — Role workspaces, guided setup and centre notices
22 November 2026 • Africa/Lagos

INSTALL
Back up matching files, database, uploads and private encryption keys. Install the trusted ZIP through Platform Super Admin → Updates & Plugins, not Plugin manager.
Sign out and back into the training demo to see the new first-use guide. The existing configured, owned sandbox prepares its training noticeboard and fictional examples automatically. Existing passwords are not reset.
For a live centre, Training Admin → Noticeboard → Enable noticeboard if prompted. This is a one-time additive preparation; no notice is published automatically in live centres. Existing cohort subscriptions and learner records are preserved.

A CLEARER WORKSPACE
Desktop: grouped sidebar, current-page heading, role badge, menu search and persistent guide access.
Mobile: Home, learning/payments, Notices, Messages and More. The full menu opens as an accessible panel with search, keyboard focus handling and Escape to close. Navigation remains available without JavaScript; chat enhancements still require JavaScript.
Menu search also understands interview, subscription, logo, sign-in and related terms. Roles see only their assigned workspaces. Existing backend permission checks remain in force. Demo settings and billing pages no longer stop rendering when the sandbox lacks a live canonical URL; live URL validation stays unchanged.

FIVE ROLE DASHBOARDS
Training Admin: real learner/cohort/application/course counts, data-based setup percentage, optional settings, application-stage pie, sign-in chart, upcoming sessions and centre notices.
To create an accountant, People & access → add Staff and grant payment-review responsibility.
Accountant: recorded learner receipts, accepted offers still unpaid, pending verification, monthly receipt chart, payment links and permitted conversations. These are recorded figures, not a bank balance.
Trainer: assigned courses, upcoming sessions, reviewed/pending attendance, learner counts, publishing status and messages.
Trainee: accessible courses, course sign-offs, reviewed attendance, certificates, upcoming sessions and personal records.
Staff: tiles follow assigned interview, follow-up, attendance, record or placement duties. Supervisor accounts retain assigned-placement and logbook scope. Unassigned admin tools are not added to their menus.
Chart figures are available as text, with clear empty states. No business traction or live operational data is fabricated; shared demos are labelled fictional.

SETUP THAT RESPONDS TO YOUR CENTRE
The admin path checks saved identity, programme, dated intake, trainer, cohort access and published course records.
When online applications are enabled, the form and sender configuration become checks. When a programme requires internships, letter branding becomes a check.
Completed core steps fold away. Extra settings remain easy to find. Saving an email sender is not proof of delivery; test an operational email through Admissions → Email delivery.

ROLE WALKTHROUGHS
Show me around opens the appropriate guide for each role, with real workspace links. First home visits offer it automatically. Reopen it anytime from the sidebar or home header.
Completion is scoped to the user and role. Shared demos keep preferences within the visitor session, so one visitor does not dismiss the guide for everyone.
Read aloud uses an available browser voice after the user chooses it. No paid speech service is required. Text remains available when speech is unsupported.

CENTRE NOTICEBOARD
Only the Training Admin publishes general or important centre-wide notices in this edition. The audience is the active admin/staff/trainers/trainees in that centre when published, including active learners without a current teaching subscription. No other school or training centre receives the notice.
Recipients explicitly acknowledge a requested notice. Simply opening it never records acknowledgement. Duplicate submissions preserve the first receipt time.
The admin can view recipient details, download the full receipt CSV, and archive notices while retaining evidence. Audience membership is captured at publication; later joiners are not retroactively added.
Published notice text is not silently edited after acknowledgement. Publish a new notice for a revised update.
In-app notice counts refresh periodically while the workspace is visible. Notices are not automatically emailed or sent through WhatsApp.

MESSAGES THAT CONNECT TO WORK
The contact directory displays permitted training contacts with role labels. Private/group chat, typing and delivery behaviour reuse the existing conversation system.
Suggested messages fill the composer, including its new-conversation form, and never send automatically. Review the draft before sending. Notice questions can carry the notice title as context. Admins can open a private reminder draft from a pending receipt, then review and send it.
Centre, teaching, active cohort, support, finance and placement relationships still determine contacts. The redesigned interface does not grant cross-centre access. School bus/library service-notice controls are excluded from training chat.

VALIDATION
Synthetic role/menu/mobile/theme checks, first-use guides, saved guide preferences, notice audience isolation, explicit acknowledgement, receipt export privacy, CSRF refusal, real two-account chat delivery and update recovery were tested. Existing school billing and affiliate payout regressions were also checked.
No real external email, WhatsApp message, payment or media broadcast was sent in tests. This ZIP is published for installation and is not automatically installed on your live site.
Once notice records exist, file-only rollback below 5.7.3 is blocked to preserve compatible acknowledgement handling. Keep matching database/files/keys for reviewed recovery.
