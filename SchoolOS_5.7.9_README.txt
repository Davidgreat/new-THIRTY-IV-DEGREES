Training Admin → Attendance → Enable attendance desk. This prepares the additive attendance tables; existing records are retained.
Select the programme cohort → Rules & calendar. Save its effective date, scheduled weekdays, arrival window, grace time, absence allowance and repeated-lateness alert settings.
People & access → give the responsible staff member Attendance officer duty. Admins can also operate the desk. A trainer does not receive attendance-officer access automatically.
The desk is opt-in by programme. Other training programmes retain their existing verified-session attendance workflow.

AUTOMATION: HOSTING CRON REQUIRED
Ask the hosting operator to run every minute:
* * * * * /path/to/php /absolute/installation/path/services_worker.php --training-attendance --limit=100
Use the host's real PHP CLI binary and actual installation path. Run alongside, not instead of, existing separate mail/stipend schedules.
The worker uses Africa/Lagos time, closes prior-day open records at 00:00 WAT, catches up scheduled absences and records a heartbeat on the Attendance screen. It sends no messages or payments.
Without this job, subsequent attendance-related requests catch up, but unattended midnight processing is not guaranteed. At scale, monitor the heartbeat and processing backlog; increase scheduling capacity when required.

PERSONAL QR AND REGISTRATION FALLBACK
A logged-in trainee opens Attendance and shows a personal QR. It expires in 90 seconds, is bound to the account/programme/day and is single-use. Refresh invalidates the old code.
The officer verifies the person's identity against their name/admission number before scanning. A successful scan is confirmed immediately; routine attendance does not await trainer review.
No shared daily secret is needed. A QR screenshot is not biometric proof; physical identity verification remains essential.
Camera decoding and QR generation are self-hosted, with no external QR service or uploaded camera frames. Camera use requires HTTPS, browser permission and a supported device.
Registration-number lookup provides a two-minute, trainee-bound check-in grant, or direct officer-recorded entry for a person without a smartphone. The grant time determines lateness, and revoked officers cannot authorise its completion.
Concurrent scans cannot consume the same token twice. Legacy daily/session actions cannot bypass a scheduled officer-managed entry day.

LATE, ABSENT AND EXCUSED
Defaults: desk opens 08:00, class starts 09:00, grace ends 09:30, check-in closes 10:00 and training ends 15:00 WAT. Configure the centre's actual hours before use.
Arrival after grace is Late. Normal entry closes at the saved cutoff. A documented after-window request can be approved by an officer after confirming physical arrival; it remains Late.
Prior/future reported absence requests may be approved as Excused, not Present.
Unexcused absences are counted only after check-in closes on configured training days, starting prospectively from the saved effective date and trainee enrolment date. Cancelled/non-training days do not count.
Recurring hours/weekdays and the treatment of late attendance for stipends are locked once records exist, preserving published attendance evidence. Calendar cancellation remains available with a reason.
Repeated-lateness alerts provide named records and a permitted private follow-up draft. A person reviews and sends the message; it is not automatically sent.

SIGN-OUT
Trainees sign themselves out without officer approval, including while on attendance hold.
Forgotten sign-outs close at next-day 00:00 WAT with an automatic flag, enabling the next day's entry.
Self-declared exit and administrative midnight closure are not proof of full-day participation or presence until midnight.

ABSENCE LIMIT AND CLEARANCE
A limit of 2 permits two unexcused absences; the third triggers the hold. A limit of 0 triggers on the first.
The dashboard shows the reason and a clearance request. Learning, new sign-in, chat and new stipend actions are blocked server-side until authorised clearance.
Existing sign-out, notices and permitted historical records remain available. Earned balances and old attendance records are not deleted. Days spent on hold do not add new absence penalties.
The authorised officer/admin records why continuation is allowed. Clearance starts a fresh allowance without rewriting history.
Only the centre administrator can screen out or restore the programme, with a recorded reason. Officers cannot reinstate a screened-out programme.

STIPENDS AND EXPORTS
New full officer-managed training periods use confirmed scheduled attendance days. Late days count only if the published rule permits. Older/mixed periods retain existing session evidence; internship confirmation rules remain separate.
Holds pause new stipend requests/scheduling/payment, preserving earned credit. Already-settled manual transfers can still be truthfully reconciled after checking their original evidence.
Register CSV downloads are Excel-readable and scoped to the selected centre, programme and date. Visible registers show up to 1,000 trainees; exports and totals include the full roster.
