- Market examples: 100, 250 and 1,000 paying schools at an assumed ₦20,000 monthly ARPA. Annualised run-rates are ₦24m, ₦60m and ₦240m respectively. These are arithmetic scenarios, not verified market counts, acquisition forecasts or current revenue.

- Pilot gates: proposed 10-school cohort, 80% core setup within seven days, 60% weekly teacher use and 30% trial-to-paid. These are hypotheses to refine, not achieved metrics or guaranteed outcomes.
- Budget example: ₦30m over 12 months with allocations of 40%, 25%, 20% and 15%. It is not the founder’s confirmed ask, an approved spend, a valuation or an investment-return promise.
