PowerPoint fonts use Open Sans; install it if your editor substitutes another font. Native slide text and objects remain editable. The HTML embeds fonts and screenshots locally. No messages are sent by the presentation; contact links open your chosen telephone/WhatsApp application.

This is a business presentation package, not a SchoolOS software update. No production school records or deployed application settings were changed.

If local-file opening is restricted by your browser policy, unzip the bundle, run `python -m http.server 8000` in its folder and open http://localhost:8000/SchoolOS_Interactive_Investor_Deck.html .
