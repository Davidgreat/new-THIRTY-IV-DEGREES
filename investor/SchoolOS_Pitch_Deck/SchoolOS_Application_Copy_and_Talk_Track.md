## One-line description
SchoolOS Nigeria is a modular school-management platform that connects school records, staff workflows and verified family relationships in one school operating environment.

## Problem
School teams often coordinate records and handovers across paper, spreadsheets and conversations. SchoolOS targets the resulting repeated entry, disconnected follow-ups and limited visibility. These are the product’s problem hypotheses; pilot research should quantify their prevalence and cost.

## Solution and product
The working product connects admissions, family onboarding, staff roles, learning, attendance, school fees and role-specific operations. Schools can activate the services they need and assign suitable access. Guided setup helps an owner start with core records and expand into HR, library, store, transport or other supported school workflows. The product has fictional role demonstrations; demonstrations are not customer traction.

## Customer and business model
The proposed initial buyer is an owner or administrator of a fee-paying Nigerian nursery, primary or secondary school. Teachers, staff, parents and pupils are the daily users. Revenue is intended to come from school-level subscriptions with monthly, four-month termly or annual billing. Actual willingness to pay, realised ARPA and cost to serve require commercial validation.

## What the next funding should prove
A scoped pilot programme should establish school activation, weekly staff usefulness, verified trial-to-paid conversion, renewal retention and support economics. Proposed targets in the deck are working hypotheses, not results. Do not state that ten schools have joined, that revenue has been earned, or that administration time has fallen without evidence.

## Why the founder is applying
SchoolOS seeks funding, pilot-school access and accelerator support to turn a working product into measured, repeatable school adoption. The amount is not yet selected. The ₦30m workbook case is a budget illustration, not a formal raise or valuation.

- How do you protect children’s data? Explain implemented access controls and school/operator responsibilities, without claiming certification or blanket compliance.
- Why this funding amount? Present a costed budget; the interactive appendix is only a scope tool.
- What would make you change the product or model? Define evidence-based gates for onboarding burden, retention and unit economics.
