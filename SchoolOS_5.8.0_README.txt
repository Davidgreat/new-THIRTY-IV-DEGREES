TWO MAIN CRON COMMANDS
Run both every minute. Leave Run at reboot unchecked. Confirm the PHP CLI binary with HostAfrica; /usr/local/bin/php is a suggestion, not verified remotely.
For the installation path shown in your file-manager screenshots:
/usr/local/bin/php /home/getschoo/domains/getschoolos.com.ng/public_html/services_worker.php --mail --limit=30
/usr/local/bin/php /home/getschoo/domains/getschoolos.com.ng/public_html/services_worker.php --training-attendance --limit=100
The dashboard generates script paths from its actual installation folder, supporting subfolder installations too.
--mail covers lifecycle, training, family handover and survey email queues. The limit applies per channel, not the combined total; survey is capped at 20 per pass. Configure sending volume for your provider's limits.
After verifying --mail, remove redundant separate --training-mail, --survey-mail and per-school --family-onboarding schedules. Existing individual modes remain supported and monitored.
Keep unrelated campaign/provider-credit, HR, school-service-period and stipend-accrual schedules as required; --mail does not replace them or send money.
Optional connection-only command: services_worker.php --cron-check. This only records a CLI/database heartbeat and sends nothing. It does not prove that email or attendance jobs work.

WHAT IS NOW CONNECTED
• New school registration → one welcome email with the sign-in address and next steps.
• Training-centre request → acknowledgement; platform approval → centre-owner welcome.
• Verified staff creation and verified administrator-assisted recovery → private password-setup email where a routable email exists. Platform-team creation/recovery is included. Existing shown-once temporary handover remains available.
• School subscription purchases, renewals and upgrades → one receipt per successful verified live transaction. Wrong/unverified/test payments do not create a production receipt. A receipt does not claim a pending allocation review is complete.
• Email password recovery → secure reset instructions; successful reset/initial password selection → password-change security notice.
• Existing training application confirmations, selected bulk interview invitations, qualified-candidate offers, accepted-offer access instructions and training payment receipts remain in the connected training queue.
• Existing approved family setup instructions and requested survey response copies are processed by the combined email worker.
Administrators still choose/approve eligible interview and offer recipients. Automation delivers the result of authorised decisions; it does not automatically qualify applicants.

SECURE ACCOUNT RECOVERY
School and training sign-in pages now include Forgot your password.
Enter the account's existing email or school sign-in ID. The response does not reveal whether an account exists.
Only active, eligible accounts with a routable registered email can receive a link. Accounts without email retain verified administrator recovery.
Recovery links are 30-minute, random, hashed in storage, account/email/password-version bound and single-use. Verified setup links expire after 24 hours.
The mail cron must deliver before expiry; otherwise request a fresh link. GET previews do not consume a link. Requests are rate-limited.
Password changes require 12–72 characters, invalidate other outstanding links and invalidate previous sessions on their next authenticated request.
Recovery does not change roles, school relationships, application approval, programme access or suspension/subscription status. It does not automatically sign the person in.

MONITOR AND TEST
Open Super Admin → Email & background jobs.
Check configuration validates database access, canonical URL and saved SMTP values. It does not connect to SMTP or claim a scheduled run.
Queue a test email sends a test into the outbox for an inbox you control. Wait for the mail cron, refresh the monitor and check both delivery status and the recipient inbox/spam.
Recent successful CLI timestamps prove that the actual command reached this database. A queue may be empty and still run successfully.
The page shows last success, last failure, running/failed/stale states and delivery counts. A healthy job can still have failed messages; inspect both.
Old individual cron jobs appear after their next run on this release. Demo databases remain separate; a live cron does not prove a demo job ran.
Only Super Admin sees platform recipient metadata. Bodies, credentials and reset links are never displayed on the monitor.

RETRIES AND PRIVACY
Lifecycle bodies are encrypted, then removed after SMTP acceptance. Accepted/cancelled metadata is retained for 90 days.
Definite lifecycle failures retry up to three times, five minutes apart. Uncertain or interrupted sends stop automatic retries; check inbox/provider logs before a controlled retry.
Expired security messages are cancelled; request fresh recovery/setup links instead of retrying old ones.
Existing training/family/survey queues keep their own explicit failure/retry rules, exposed on their existing delivery pages.
SMTP acceptance is not proof of inbox placement. Back up the matching private key and database; missing-key protection refuses to silently generate a replacement over protected records.
