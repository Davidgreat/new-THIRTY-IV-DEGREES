SchoolOS Nigeria — 90-second platform introduction

Main deliverable: SchoolOS_Nigeria_Introduction_90s_1080p.mp4
Full HD 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps. English voiceover with a Nigerian-accent synthetic voice, original generated instrumental music and click accents, burned-in captions, animated dashboard scenes and CTA.

The film combines existing SchoolOS demonstration screenshots with promotional interface illustrations. Schools, pupils, totals and reports shown are fictional. Illustrative scenes communicate existing features; they are not recordings of a production school or promises of earnings/academic outcomes.

Additional files:
- SchoolOS_Introduction_Captions.srt: timed captions for editors/platforms.
- SchoolOS_Introduction_Audio.m4a: separate final narration/music mix.
- SchoolOS_Introduction_Transcript.txt: narration and scene timing.
- SchoolOS_Introduction_Storyboard.jpg: nine-scene visual overview.
- SchoolOS_Introduction_Editable_Project.zip: HTML Canvas animation, assets and audio. Unzip, run `python -m http.server 8000` in the project folder, then open http://localhost:8000/preview.html and press Play. Edit film.js/scenes.json to revise visuals or copy. Production MP4 is the ready-to-share file.

No SchoolOS application files or deployed website settings were changed to make this video. The footage is an introduction, not an installation update. No real pupil information or bank details are included.
